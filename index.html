Good to know

Questions salon owners ask us

Do I need to send you photos and videos?

Phone photos are fine — most of our best-performing content is shot at the desk between clients. Send them in one WhatsApp thread and we handle the editing, design and captions. If you'd like a proper shoot, we can arrange one.

Is the ad spend included in the monthly fee?

No. Our service fee covers building and managing the campaigns. The ad budget itself is paid by you directly to Meta or Google, so you stay in control of it and can see exactly where it goes.

Is the website really free?

On Pro and Elite, yes — design, hosting and domain are included when you stay for 12 months. You can cancel any time; if you cancel inside the first year, a one-off build fee applies. The exact figure is listed with the packages above.

How does the TV signage work in the salon?

We design a slideshow of your services, offers and best work. It plays from a USB stick in the TV you already have, or from a YouTube link — no special hardware. It's included on Elite with yearly payment, or you can take it on its own; the à la carte figures sit with the packages above.

Am I locked into a contract?

Packages are billed monthly and you can stop any time. The only commitment is the 12-month term attached to a free website build.

Do you only work with UK salons?

Today, yes — all our packages are priced and built for the UK market. Australia and Canada are next; tell us where you are and we'll let you know when we open there.

What languages do you work in?

We work in English and Vietnamese, so briefing us is easy whichever you're more comfortable with. Your content is published in English for your clients.
